## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Planning documents -> Executor | Process-phase artifact content (audit scores, sign-off records) consumed by downstream tools (`/gsd-complete-milestone`, `/gsd-new-milestone`). Malformed artifacts could cause incorrect milestone transitions. |
| Human sign-off -> Archive | The human sign-off signal is a typed response in a chat interface. A false-positive approval (mistyped "approved" without review) would skip the attestation gate. |

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-25-01 | Spoofing | Sign-off checkpoint | low | accept | The human types "approved" to confirm. Risk is low because the intent is to get actual human consent; a mistype is indistinguishable from genuine approval at this automation level.
